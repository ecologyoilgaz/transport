How to Fund a Solution

  1. Go to the Tokens & Pay page.
  2. Choose your token (€ECO 1, €ECO 2, €GREEN, or a specific one like €20).
  3. Send it to our Treasury wallet.
  4. In the Memo, write the Project ID (e.g., MEMO: almaty-transport).
