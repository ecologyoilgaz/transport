How It Works (The Flow)

  1. You Send Funds: You send $100 USDT to our Treasury and receive 10 €ECO tokens.
  2. You Tag a Project: In the transaction memo, write the ID of the solution you want to fund (e.g., MEMO: almaty-transport).
  3. We Execute: Funds are released to the project.
  4. Satellite Verification: We check satellite data. If impact is confirmed, status changes.
  5. Your Token Updates: The €ECO tokens in your wallet become a permanent ESG certificate.
